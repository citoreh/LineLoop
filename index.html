❓ How Line Loop works

Draw lines, hear music. Up and down is pitch (higher = higher note), left to right is time. A playhead sweeps across and every line becomes an instrument in a repeating loop.

Each sample loads and starts playing. Then try changing things — everything below can be adjusted while it plays.

Quick start

  1. Pick an instrument (the coloured circles) and draw a line with your finger or mouse. You hear it as you draw.
  2. Press ▶ Play. Draw more lines — each one is another instrument.
  3. Change the Scale or Beat, then download it as WAV + MIDI, a Video or Sheet music.

⬇ WAV + MIDI
A .zip with: .wav audio (exactly what you hear), .mid (one track per line + drums + 808, opens in GarageBand, Logic, Ableton, FL Studio…), .musicxml sheet music, and .json (your drawing).
16:9 · 1:1 · 9:16 + ⬇ Video
Records the loop with its sound as an MP4 — widescreen, square (Instagram feed) or vertical (Reels/Stories/TikTok). It records in real time, so keep the page visible. Firefox makes WebM instead of MP4.
🎼 Sheet
Shows your loop as sheet music, one stave per line, notes rounded to 1/16. Download as MusicXML (MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale, Dorico) + SVG. Persian quarter-tones are written with koron / sori.

✏️ Instruments & editing

✏️ Draw / 👆 Select
Draw makes new lines. Select lets you tap a line to edit it: tap an instrument to change its sound, pick a colour, drag it to move it (sideways = when, up/down = pitch), or 🗑 Delete it. Keyboard: Delete, Esc to finish.
Instruments
Sine, Triangle, Square, Saw (classic synths) · Organ · Ney (breathy flute) · Strings · Bass (an octave lower) · Bell · Pluck · Santur (hammered, with tremolo) · Choir.
Colour · Auto
Choose the colour of the next line (or of the selected line). Auto uses the instrument's own colour.

🎚 Sound & timing

Scale
Which notes the lines play: Major / Minor pentatonic (always sounds nice), Major, Minor, Dorian, Shur (Persian, with the koron quarter-tone second) or Chromatic (every note).
Snap
On: lines play clean notes from the scale. Off: they glide smoothly, exactly following your hand — great for signatures and sweeping curves.
Beat
Basic kick + hats · Boom bap (90 bpm, swung) · Trap (140 bpm, hi-hat rolls) · Lo-fi (78 bpm, soft) · Off. Hip-hop beats add an 808 bass that follows your lowest line, plus extra kicks, ghost snares and hat rolls shaped by your drawing.
⇄ Reverse
Plays right-to-left — handy for Persian/Arabic text so it plays in reading order.
🎹 Keys
Shows the piano keyboard on the left (keys light up as they play), note names next to the playhead dots, and the chord name at the top.
✳ Crossings
One: a line plays one note at a time. All: where a line loops back (a signature's "b", a crossed "t"), every crossing sounds together as a chord.
Tempo · Bars · Echo
Speed in beats per minute · loop length (1–8 bars of 4 beats) · amount of echo.

🖼 Images

🖼 Image
Load a picture (or drop one on the canvas). It's shown faintly behind the grid and traced into lines automatically.
Trace
Ink — follows the middle of each pen stroke; best for signatures, handwriting, calligraphy, line drawings. Dots (on an i, full stops) become short notes. · Outline — follows edges; good for shapes, logos, photos. · Melody bands — splits the picture into horizontal strips and follows the strongest marks; smooth tunes from busy photos.
Detail · Voices · Fit
Detail 1–5: more keeps every wiggle, less gives a simpler melody. Voices: the most instruments to use. Fit: crop to the drawing so it fills the canvas.
Colour → instrument
Every colour gets its own sound — red → Organ, orange → Square, yellow → Bell, green → Pluck, cyan → Sine, blue → Bass/Choir, violet → Strings, magenta → Santur, pink → Saw. Black/grey lines are chosen by height (high → Santur/Bell, middle → Ney/Strings, low → Bass/Choir). A line that changes colour is split.
Re-trace · Show · ✕
Trace again with new settings · fade the picture in/out · remove it.

✍️ Text

Text box · Font · Bold
Type a word or name, choose a font, press Enter or ✍️ Make lines. Fonts include ones found on your device (Persian ones too, e.g. B Nazanin, IRANSans, Vazir) and free web fonts (Vazirmatn, Lalezar, Nastaliq, Amiri, Great Vibes…). Script fonts + Ink tracing sound especially good.
🔍 My fonts
Lists every font on your computer (Chrome or Edge, in the standalone file only).

📷 Camera

📷 Camera · Draw with · Show
Draw in the air with your index finger. Pinch (thumb + index together) or Point (index only) to put the pen down; open your hand to lift it. Claude blocks cameras inside its pages, so use the standalone line-loop.html file in Chrome or Safari.
